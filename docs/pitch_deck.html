● AJMAN DIGITAL GROWTH · CONTEXT

Real DED numbers. Since 2018 new licenses rose from 4,858 to 7,662 while exits fell from 5,338 to 3,074. Exits = last year’s active + new − this year’s active, so quiet non-renewals are included. The question for DED: where will growth come from next, and how do we keep it?
